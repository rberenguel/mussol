How to Play

The goal is to find the analogy. Read the first pair of words and understand their relationship.

"A is to B..."

Then, select the answer from the four choices that has the most similar relationship. Some might be weird, this is using AI training datasets!

  • The score is tracked at the top right.
  • Press the icon to restart the quiz and reset the score.
  • Press the icon to skip a question.
